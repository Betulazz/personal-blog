# 08: Homepage motion and viewport acceptance

**What to build:** Turn the composed homepage into an approved Phase 3 prototype through purposeful motion, responsive correction, and browser-based visual acceptance.

**Blocked by:** 06: Homepage content aggregation; 07: Cross-route music player.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Entry, reveal, hover, navigation, and player motion clarify state without delaying content or producing excessive simultaneous movement.
- [ ] Reduced-motion and mobile modes remove nonessential animation and expensive background behavior.
- [ ] Viewport and separate full-page screenshots are inspected at every fixed comparison size.
- [ ] Navigation, player, all homepage states, keyboard use, touch targets, and content overflow work.
- [ ] Console output and a lightweight runtime/layer inspection show no material errors or obvious performance regression.
- [ ] Phase 3 acceptance evidence is recorded without committing temporary screenshots.
