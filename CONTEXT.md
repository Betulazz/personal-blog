# Domain Glossary

## Content

**Post**  
A formal long-form publication with a title, summary, canonical Markdown body, publication state, category, tags, and reading metadata. A Post is intended to remain useful and discoverable over time.

**Chatter**  
A short-form but composed note for ideas, technical fragments, or reflections that are more structured than a Moment and less formal than a Post. Chatter has its own title or lead, body, tags, time, and optional mood.

**Moment**  
A lightweight life update centered on a timestamp and short body, optionally accompanied by images, location, mood, and tags. A Moment is feed-oriented rather than article-oriented.

**Category**  
A primary editorial grouping for Posts. A Post belongs to at most one Category.

**Tag**  
A reusable descriptive label used to connect content across narrower topics. Tags are entities, not comma-separated text.

## Portfolio and relationships

**Project**  
A portfolio entry describing a piece of work, its status, technology, time span, and relevant source or demonstration links.

**Friend Link**  
A curated outbound link to another personal site, with a name, avatar, description, and destination. It does not imply live availability monitoring.

## Site identity and media

**Site Identity**  
The configurable public presentation of the blog owner and site, including name, descriptions, avatar, social links, footer information, and default metadata.

**Media Asset**  
An image or audio object managed by the site and referenced by content or settings. A Media Asset has a stable logical identity independent of its storage provider.

**Music Track**  
An administrator-configured audio item with display metadata and a legal media source. It is part of the site's ambient experience, not a third-party music catalog mirror.

## People and access

**Administrator**  
A trusted person allowed to manage content, media, and site settings. The first Administrator is deliberately created during deployment; no default Administrator exists.
