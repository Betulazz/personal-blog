# Editorial Manga Field Notes

The public blog uses one visual language with two levels of energy. The entrance is an editorial manga spread; the reading surfaces settle into a quiet field notebook. The administrator workspace keeps its existing visual treatment.

## Visual grammar

- **Ink and paper:** warm ivory `#f4f1e8` and near-black `#222720` carry the interface. Raised paper is `#fffdf6`. Vermilion `#cc392d` marks actions and chapter cues; botanical green `#566d55` supports secondary information. Avoid purple gradients and colored glass.
- **Typography:** large Chinese serif display headlines create the opening impression. Smaller serif body copy and compact sans or mono metadata create a slower reading rhythm. Long-form content stays within a 45rem measure and uses generous line height.
- **Composition:** use two-pixel ink rules, manga framing, asymmetric editorial columns and numbered chapters on the first screen. After the first screen, use one-pixel rules, open margins and fewer framed regions. Images lead cards and article details; metadata follows the image.
- **Annotations:** short marginal notes and folio labels can make the page feel personal. They should support the information hierarchy, not become decorative paragraphs. Botanical cues stay faint and sparse.
- **Motion:** quick page entry, restrained image hover and a small click ripple. Reading and navigation do not depend on animation; reduced-motion preferences remain supported.

## Page rhythm

1. **Home opening:** a manga panel, oversized sentence, sparse annotation and clear reading action establish the identity.
2. **Home content and collections:** numbered section headers, paper cards, natural color and visible whitespace lower the contrast. Search controls sit in a compact line near the collection heading.
3. **Article detail:** the cover opens the article. Title, metadata, table of contents and prose follow in that order, with a narrow text measure.
4. **About:** a divided illustration and introduction lead into a profile rail and a calm document flow. The rail becomes a single column on mobile.
5. **Music and auxiliary pages:** use the same paper, ink, red action and green secondary colors without repeating the large manga hero.

## Practical rules

- Keep covers above preview text. Give remote images a quiet fallback so failed media does not become an empty white panel.
- Keep the floating player compact by default, with an explicit expansion control for track details. Its controls remain reachable by keyboard and touch.
- Preserve data-driven copy and interaction paths. Editorial text can be rewritten, while API-backed titles, summaries, links and state messages remain honest.
- Treat public and administrator themes separately until the administrator redesign is requested.
